01 Vegetarian

Chowpatty Pani Puri Shots

Pani Puri / Golgappa · Sooji puris · spiced aloo-chana · pudina & imli pani, served as shots

Pani puri is one of India’s best-loved street snacks. In Mumbai it became a fixture of Chowpatty Beach, where vendors still serve it one puri at a time.

On the plate

  • 6 frosted shot glasses in a straight line on a long matte-black slate.
  • Alternate glasses with pudina pani and pudina-imli layered pani for two colours.
  • Puri sits on the rim, filling up, with sev and one micro coriander leaf.
  • A small brass bowl of extra imli pani at one end of the slate.

AllergensGluten. Confirm every allergy with your server.

02 Vegetarian

Malai Moti Dahi Puri

Dahi Batata Puri · Crisp puris · chilled meethi dahi · saunth & hari chutney · nylon sev

Dahi batata puri is a Mumbai street classic, born at the chaat stalls of Girgaum and Chowpatty. The name ‘Malai Moti’ (creamy pearls) refers to the six round puris coated in chilled dahi.

On the plate

  • Six puris in a straight line on a long oval white porcelain plate.
  • Neat dots of saunth and hari chutney between the puris.
  • A crown of nylon sev and ruby pomegranate on each puri.

AllergensGluten, dairy. Confirm every allergy with your server.

03 Vegetarian

Purani Dilli Papdi Chaat

Papdi Chaat · Papdi · aloo · kabuli chana · dahi · saunth & pudina chutney

Papdi chaat belongs to the lanes of Old Delhi around Chandni Chowk. A popular legend says chaat was created in Shah Jahan’s 17th-century Delhi, when spiced, tangy foods were thought to protect against the city’s water.

On the plate

  • Wide, shallow matte-cream bowl.
  • Papdi fanned in a circle so the edges stand up through the dahi.
  • Zig-zag lines of the two chutneys; sev piled in the centre.

AllergensGluten, dairy. Confirm every allergy with your server.

04 Vegetarian

Juhu Beach Bhel

Bhel Puri · Murmura · sev · kairi · pyaz-tamatar · teekhi-meethi chutney

Bhel puri is Mumbai’s beach snack, sold at sunset on Juhu and Chowpatty. It is mixed to order because puffed rice goes soft within minutes once the chutneys are added.

On the plate

  • Packed into a ring mould on a white plate for a tall, neat stack; the ring is lifted.
  • Crowned with sev and fine kairi matchsticks; one whole papdi shard tucked in upright.
  • For a playful touch, a small newspaper-print paper cone on the side.

AllergensGluten, peanuts. Confirm every allergy with your server.

05 Vegetarian

Samosa Shringar Chaat

Samosa Chaat · Crushed samosa · Pindi chole · dahi · imli & hari chutney

The samosa came to India with Central Asian traders as the ‘sambosa’ and is described in court writings by the 13th–14th century. ‘Shringar’ means adornment: the humble samosa, dressed up.

On the plate

  • Shallow black stoneware bowl so the chole shows dark against it.
  • Samosa pieces arranged so the pastry triangle points and filling both show.
  • A single swoosh of dahi across one side; chutney dots on the rim.

AllergensGluten, dairy. Confirm every allergy with your server.

06 Vegetarian

Dilli Dil Tikki

Aloo Tikki Chaat · Heart-shaped aloo tikki · chole · meethi dahi · anardana

Aloo tikki chaat is a Delhi and Uttar Pradesh street favourite, shallow-fried on huge iron tawas until the crust is deep brown. Karva shapes the tikkis as hearts: ‘Dilli ka dil’, the heart of Delhi.

On the plate

  • Matte-white round plate.
  • Two heart tikkis overlapping on a pool of chole.
  • Dahi spooned only on the centre of each heart so the crust stays visible.

AllergensDairy. Confirm every allergy with your server.

07 Vegetarian

Hass & Hing Chaat

Avocado Chaat, a Karva creation · Hing-tadka avocado · papdi · sev · chukandar & imli chutney

A Karva creation that brings the guacamole of Texas to the chaat tradition of North India. A hot tadka of mustard oil and hing (asafoetida) gives the soft avocado a savoury, chaat-shop depth.

On the plate

  • Pale grey stone plate.
  • A bold magenta beetroot stroke as the base.
  • Three green avocado quenelles, each with two papdi standing upright like wings.

AllergensGluten, mustard. Confirm every allergy with your server.

08 Vegetarian

Palak Patta Chaat

Crispy Spinach Chaat · Crisp palak patta · dahi · imli-pudina chutney · sev

Palak patta chaat grew out of the North Indian pakora tradition: whole spinach leaves dipped in light besan batter and fried until shatteringly crisp. It was made famous by Delhi’s modern Indian restaurants in the 2000s.

On the plate

  • Large flat white plate.
  • Leaves stacked loosely so they look light and airy, not flattened.
  • Dahi and chutneys in fine lines across the stack.

AllergensDairy. Confirm every allergy with your server.

09 Vegetarian

Shakarkandi Chaat Salad

Shakarkandi Chaat · Grilled shakarkandi · anardana glaze · chaat masala · roomali crisps

Every Delhi winter, street vendors roast shakarkandi (sweet potato) on coals, then slice it and toss it with lemon and chaat masala. Karva serves it as a warm salad, glazed with tangy pomegranate and topped with crisp shards of roomali roti.

On the plate

  • Wide cream plate.
  • A thin swoosh of hari chutney as the base.
  • Glazed sweet potato in a loose pile, roomali crisps standing upright between the pieces.

AllergensGluten, peanuts, dairy (ghee). Confirm every allergy with your server.

10 Vegetarian

Rajasthani Pyaaz Ki Kachoori

Pyaaz Kachori · Crisp onion kachori · pulled jackfruit · achari aloo gravy

Pyaaz kachori is the pride of Jodhpur, where famous sweet shops fry thousands every morning. Karva serves the kachori with a tangy achari (pickle-spiced) potato gravy and spiced pulled jackfruit.

On the plate

  • Small copper or brass-rimmed bowl.
  • Achari gravy as a pool; the kachori in the centre, cracked open at the top with jackfruit spilling out.
  • Pickled onion rings, saunth dots and coriander on the kachori.

AllergensGluten, mustard, dairy (ghee). Confirm every allergy with your server.

11 Vegetarian

Mini Raj Kachoori

Raj Kachori · Mini raj kachori · khatta-meetha pumpkin · saunth chutney foam

Raj kachori, the ‘royal’ kachori, is a giant puffed shell filled with everything a chaat stall has to offer. Karva serves three bite-size versions, each filled with sweet-sour pumpkin and topped with a light saunth chutney foam.

On the plate

  • Three mini kachoris in a triangle on a round white plate.
  • Each crowned with a cloud of pale brown saunth foam.
  • Pumpkin cubes and pomegranate scattered around the base.

AllergensGluten, dairy, soy (lecithin). Confirm every allergy with your server.

12 Vegetarian

Avocado Dahi Poori

Dahi Puri with Tadka Guacamole · Semolina puris · tadka guac · sweet curd

A Karva twist on Mumbai’s dahi puri. In place of potato, each puri holds a ‘tadka guac’: avocado tempered with cumin, mustard seed and curry leaves in hot mustard oil.

On the plate

  • Six puris on a long rectangular cream plate, two rows of three.
  • A crisp fried curry leaf on top of each puri.
  • Green guac visible at the opening under the white dahi.

AllergensGluten, dairy, mustard. Confirm every allergy with your server.

13 Vegetarian

Nachos Paapdi Chaat

Indo-Mex Papdi Chaat, a Karva creation · Round tortilla chips · spiced cheese sauce · pico de gallo

A playful Karva creation for Texas. It crosses Old Delhi’s papdi chaat with Tex-Mex nachos: corn tortilla chips take the place of papdi, a chaat-spiced cheese sauce stands in for part of the dahi, and pico de gallo meets the familiar chaat chutneys.

On the plate

  • Round black cast-iron skillet-style plate on a wooden board.
  • Chips fanned in a circle; cheese and dahi spooned in the centre.
  • Pico de gallo and sev piled in the middle.

AllergensDairy, gluten (sev, flour). Confirm every allergy with your server.

14 Vegetarian

Kamal Kakkdi Chaat

Crispy Lotus Stem Chaat · Crisp lotus stem · trio of chutneys · sesame crunch

Kamal kakdi (lotus stem) is prized in Kashmir, where it is called nadru, and in Sindhi cooking, where it is called beeh. Its lace-like cross-section looks beautiful when sliced thin.

On the plate

  • Long white plate.
  • Three parallel chutney strokes: green, brown, magenta.
  • Lotus stem piled high across the strokes so the lacy holes show.

AllergensSesame. Confirm every allergy with your server.

15 Vegetarian

Panchmel Dal Mathri

Panchmel Dal with Mathri · Crisp mathri · tempered five-lentil dal · kachumber salsa

Panchmel dal, made from five lentils, is the classic partner to baati in Rajasthan’s royal kitchens of Mewar. Karva serves the thick, smoky dal as a warm chaat with mathri for scooping and a fresh kachumber salsa.

On the plate

  • Small cast-iron or brass handi bowl on a wooden board.
  • Thick dal with a glossy ghee-chilli tadka on top and a mound of kachumber in the centre.
  • Five mathri tucked upright around one side of the bowl.

AllergensGluten, dairy (ghee). Confirm every allergy with your server.
